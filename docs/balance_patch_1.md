# Balance Patch: March 2026

## Protocol Amendments

### Bridges
- Scaling increased to 5% (up from 1%).
- Cost increased to 20 Ti (up from 10 Ti).

### Builder Bots
- Self-destruct damage removed.
- Can now attack: 2 Ti for 2 damage, only on the tile it’s standing on.
- Healing changed to 1 Ti for 4 HP (down from 0 Ti for 10 HP).
- Cost increased to 50 Ti (up from 10 Ti).
- Scaling increased to 20% (up from 10%).

### Sentinels
- Scaling increased to 20% (up from 10%).
- Reload time changed to 2 rounds.
- Damage set to 10.
- Ammo per shot set to 5.

### Global Changes
- Global cap of 50 units per team.
- Raw Axionite is now destroyed when given to the core or turrets.

### Breach
- Vision radius² increased to 13 (up from 10).

## Rationale

- Bridges were objectively better than conveyors, so they needed a meaningful downside.
- Removing builder self-destruct nerfs rush, while adding a weak attack stops builders from becoming totally helpless.
- Builder bot healing needed to be weaker so normal attacking is actually viable.
- Higher unit scaling, more expensive builders, and a global unit cap all help reduce spam and improve runtime.
- Destroying raw Axionite at the core and turrets stops players from mining it mindlessly without refining it.
- The Sentinel change stops two Sentinels from instantly one-shotting a builder bot now that builders are much more expensive.
- A breach could previously hit a target with splash that it could not see.
